NSoft Agent Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NSoft Agent collects, uses, stores, and protects information when users use the NSoft Agent mobile application.

1. Information We Process

NSoft Agent may process information necessary for users to access and use the application's business features, including account information and work-related information provided through the application.

2. Location Information

Background Location: NSoft Agent may collect location information while an employee's work session and GPS tracking are active, including when the app is running in the background or is not actively being used.

Location information is used for business purposes such as:

  • recording customer visits;
  • recording an agent's work route;
  • confirming visits to customer locations;
  • supporting location-based work activities;
  • displaying work routes and visit history to authorized users.

Location tracking is used only as part of the application's work-related functionality.

3. How Information Is Used

Information processed by NSoft Agent may be used to:

  • authenticate users;
  • provide application functionality;
  • manage orders, customers and related business operations;
  • record work activity and customer visits;
  • provide location and route functionality;
  • maintain security and reliability of the application.

4. Data Sharing

Information is used for providing the application's business functionality and may be accessible to authorized administrators or other authorized users of the organization using NSoft Agent.

Personal information is not sold for advertising purposes.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Communication with supported servers is performed using secure network connections where applicable.

6. Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to provide the application's functionality, satisfy business requirements, and meet applicable legal or operational requirements.

7. Data Deletion

Users or organizations may request deletion of applicable personal information by contacting the developer using the contact information below.

8. Permissions

Depending on enabled features, NSoft Agent may request permissions such as location and notifications.

Location permissions, including background location where required, are used for agent route tracking and customer visit functionality.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality, legal requirements, or data practices change.
